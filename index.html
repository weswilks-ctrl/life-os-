Executive Summary

Financial Independence, Property Growth & a Business of Your Own

Wes — Leading Hand Chef, Royal Navy  ·  August 2026 – August 2031

You're in a strong starting position: a stable Navy salary with food and accommodation covered, one buy-to-let already cash-flowing positively, several active investment accounts, and a side business you're actively building skills toward. This plan turns those into a five-year route toward financial security, a second BTL property, a third income stream, and the stability to start a family on your terms.

Why this plan is structured the way it is: most people try to save, invest, pay down debt and build a business all at once, in no particular order — and progress stalls. This plan sequences those goals deliberately (emergency fund → clear short debt → deposit fund → business proof points → purchase → re-evaluate), so each step makes the next one safer, not riskier.
